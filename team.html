Project selection

Track
Track F: Escape Room Challenge
Setting
An abandoned laboratory
Pack
Investigation Pack
Graduate feature 1
Assistant panels with prepared gentle hints and full explanations.
Graduate feature 2
Recommendation panels that suggest a next step with a reason and confidence label.

Implementation

The website uses HTML and CSS. Radio selections control puzzle feedback and recommendations, while expandable records reveal evidence and hints.

The assistant and recommendation features are prepared simulations. They do not connect to an AI service. Puzzle selections do not transfer between pages.

Development assistance and assets

OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex assisted with planning, and implementation guidance.

The design uses system fonts and CSS-created visuals, without external image or audio assets.
