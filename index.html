Lab 13: The Lockdown

The laboratory has been abandoned, but its security system is still active. Investigate three puzzle stations, examine the remaining evidence, and uncover the answers needed to open the exit.

Start the investigation How to play

How to play

  1. Visit the puzzle rooms and read each station's instructions.
  2. Use the clue board to examine related evidence.
  3. Request a hint when you need help.
  4. Write down the answer from each completed puzzle.
  5. Choose your three answers on the final unlock page.

Keep your own notes. Puzzle selections do not automatically transfer between pages.
